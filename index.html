The Story

When a warrior falls, their soul does not rest. It wakes in Udaya, the realm between death and ascension, a quiet hall of spirits where every fallen fighter carries one hope: to be reborn into their own flesh and blood. The path back is long. Five worlds stand as stepping stones, from the rooted tombs of Vanastha to the sanctum of Swargadwar, and only a soul strong enough to cross them all will walk again.

The Four Tribes

Tank / Damager

Rudra The Unbroken

In life, the Rudra were the wall that never fell: protectors who held the line so others could live. When the last gate broke, a Rudra warrior stood alone in its ruins and kept fighting long after their shield had shattered. They fell holding that ground, and their spirit woke in Udaya still gripping the oath. A Rudra seeks rebirth for one reason: a promise to protect is not finished just because the body was lost.

Assassin / Hunter

Vetala The Unseen

The Vetala lived between shadow and moonlight: hunters who never missed, blades that were felt before they were seen. They took the hardest marks, the ones no one else dared to follow, and one hunt led a Vetala into a trap meant for them all along. Struck down by a hand they never saw, they woke in Udaya with the hunt still burning in their spirit. Now they track the one prey that matters: the path back to their own flesh and blood.

Support / Warrior

Siddha The Accomplished

The Siddha were masters of balance, as ready to mend a wound as to open one. On the battlefield they moved between the fallen, carrying the wounded with one hand and their weapon in the other. A Siddha gave the last of their strength to save their companions and fell among them before the battle ended. In Udaya they still walk the middle path, for a soul that learned to give life knows best how to earn it back.

Mage / Summoner

Maya The Illusionist

The Maya bent the world with their will: illusion, fire, frost and the spirits that answered their call. A Maya reached for a power greater than any before, and the spell that should have saved their people consumed its caster instead. Their body was lost, but their mind crossed into Udaya awake and unafraid. To them the realm is just another veil to see through, and beyond it waits the flesh they mean to reclaim.

The Game

  • Real-time auto-battle in dark sealed halls, one for each world, where your soul is the brightest light.
  • Keeps fighting while you're away. Close the app and your character keeps hunting; come back to the rewards.
  • Four tribes, eight jobs, each with its own weapons and signature skills.
  • Bosses that hunt you back: mini-bosses and world bosses crowned with horns.
  • Guilds, Arena 1v1 and 5v5, Guild War, weekend tournaments, Nobility and a Trade Hall.
  • Gear to chase: six rarities, the Forge, gems, awakened stats and tribe sets.
