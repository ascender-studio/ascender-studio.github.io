This policy explains what information Karma Path: Soul Ascent collects, why it is collected, and what you can do about it. The game is developed and operated by Ascender Studio ("we", "us").

Summary

  • We collect your Google account email, display name and account identifier so you can sign in.
  • We store your characters, progress and in-game activity so the game works across devices.
  • Messages you send to other players are stored so they can be delivered and moderated.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking software.
  • We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • We do not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Information we collect

Account information

Karma Path requires sign-in with a Google account. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We receive and store:

  • Your email address
  • Your Google display name
  • A unique account identifier assigned by Firebase

We never receive your Google password.

Game data

To run the game and keep your progress, we store:

  • Character names, tribe, gender choice, job, level, experience and allocated stats
  • Inventory, equipment, currencies (Mudra, Ratna), Karma Points and reward boxes
  • Guild, arena team and friend-list membership
  • Nobility rank and standings
  • Participation and results in Arena, Guild War, Karma Clash, tournaments and poker tables
  • Amounts staked and won in in-game event guessing

Communications

Karma Path includes world chat, guild chat, direct messages and the Trade Hall. Messages and posts you send are stored on our servers so they can be delivered to other players and reviewed if reported. Do not share personal information in chat — other players can read it.

We also store records created by safety features: players you have blocked, and reports you submit about other players or their content.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card details, bank information or billing address. We receive a confirmation from Google that a purchase was completed, which we use to grant the corresponding items, currency or subscription to your account.

Technical information

Our servers automatically record basic technical data when the app connects, such as the time of a request and the account making it. This is a normal part of operating an online service and is used to keep the game running and to investigate abuse.

What we do not collect

  • No location data. The app does not request location permission. Game "worlds" are fictional areas, not places.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising software.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting software.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, files or other apps.

The only Android permission the app requests is internet access.

Legal bases (for players in the EEA and UK)

  • Contract — processing needed to provide the game you have asked to play, including your account, progress and multiplayer features.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the game secure, preventing cheating and abuse, and handling reports.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain records or respond to lawful requests.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. Information is shared only with:

  • Google (Firebase and Google Play) — our service provider for authentication, data storage, server functions and payment processing. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own terms.
  • Other players — your character name, tribe, level, job, Nobility rank, guild, online status and anything you choose to post in chat or the Trade Hall are visible to other players in the game. Your email address is never shown to other players.
  • Authorities — where we are legally required to do so.

Where your data is stored

Karma Path's servers are hosted by Google in Singapore. If you play from another country, your information is transferred to and stored in Singapore. Where required, these transfers rely on Google Cloud's standard contractual clauses and equivalent safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Account and game data — kept while your account exists. Deleted when you delete your account (see below).
  • Chat messages and Trade Hall posts — kept for a limited period so conversations remain readable and reports can be investigated. World chat retains the most recent messages only.
  • Reports and moderation records — kept after account deletion where needed to enforce our rules and protect other players.
  • Purchase records — retained as required for tax and accounting purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Provide a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Restrict or object to certain processing

Email theworldquiz23+support@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Deleting your account

You can request deletion of your account and its data at any time. See Account Deletion for how to do this and what is removed.

Age requirement

Karma Path: Soul Ascent is intended for players aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is stored on Google's infrastructure and transmitted over encrypted connections. Access to player data is restricted to the developer. No online service can promise perfect security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game develops. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
