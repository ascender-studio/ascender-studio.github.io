Last updated: 14 November 2026

You can delete your Karma Path: Soul Ascent account and its data at any time. This page explains how, and what is removed.

How to request deletion

By email

Send an email to theworldquiz23+support@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request".

Send it from the Google account you use to sign in to the game. This is how we confirm the request is yours. Include your character name or names if you know them.

We will confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account record and sign-in identity
  • Your email address and display name
  • All characters, levels, experience and allocated stats
  • All inventory, equipment, accessories, stones and gems
  • All currency balances — Mudra, Ratna and Karma Points
  • Reward boxes and unclaimed rewards
  • Guild, arena team and friendship records tied to your account
  • Nobility seats held by your characters
  • Your direct messages
  • Your blocked-player list

What is kept, and for how long

A small amount of information is kept after deletion. This is either required by law or necessary to protect other players.

WhatWhyHow long
Purchase and transaction records Required for tax and accounting law As required by law, typically up to 7 years
Reports made about you, and moderation decisions To enforce the rules and protect other players from repeat behaviour Up to 2 years
Public chat and Trade Hall messages you posted They form part of other players' conversations Until they expire normally; your name is removed where possible
Completed trades with other players The other party's inventory record depends on them Retained in anonymised form
Historical event and tournament results Other players' records and rankings depend on them Retained in anonymised form

Important things to know

  • Deletion is permanent. Characters and progress cannot be restored afterwards, even if you sign up again with the same Google account.
  • Unused currency is not refunded. Any remaining Mudra, Ratna or Karma Points are lost.
  • Cancel your subscription separately. Deleting your game account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions, or you will continue to be billed.
  • Guild leadership. If you lead a guild or arena team, transfer leadership before deleting, or it will pass automatically or the group will be dissolved.
  • Uninstalling is not deleting. Removing the app from your device does not delete your account data.

Questions

If you are unsure about anything, email theworldquiz23+support@gmail.com before sending a deletion request.
